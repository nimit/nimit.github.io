### 2025-09-03 - Blogging when AI

Writing blogs in the age of AI, spending 4+ hours forming my thoughts into words and sentences when even local LLMs can spit out similar writing in a fraction of the time seems like a fool's errand.
But, I look at it in a more positive way. In the world of advanced, genetically modified, pest-free and more visually appealing produce, the public would pay a premium for products borne out of farming "the old way". Just like organic food commands a premium, scarcity creates opportunities for birthing things for which a select few can develop a taste.

Back on the ground, writing blogs has a few real advantages that I think make it worth the time spent.

- It allows me to practice enunciating my thoughts. Communication is always important regardless of the career (then again, maybe we are moving towards a future where everyone has an AI rewriter attached to all input devices and everyone speaks in broken sentences just trying to make the model understand - a future where no one talks normally...).

- I can share my experiences in a public setting hoping others notice me (especially important at the start of my career) and a few learn from it (hopefully!)
- I can look at how my thoughts/writing changed over time and that's f\*\*king cool!
- I can polish my creativity and get ready for conversations while I gather enough courage to leave this dungeon.

I encourage everyone to write a few blogs (if only just to give me interesting stuff to look at).
